Cider Sizing Guide: Does Cider Clothing Run Small?

Published: September 2026 | Reviewed by Vantage Retail Specialists

Shopping at Cider (Shopcider) is one of the easiest ways to refresh your wardrobe with the latest aesthetic, vintage-inspired, and viral TikTok trends. But like many global e-commerce platforms, finding your perfect size online can feel like a guessing game.

One of the most frequent questions from new buyers is: "Does Cider run small?" Because Cider's designs range from ultra-stretchy ribbed knits to rigid, highly structured denim, the answer depends heavily on the fabric and collection. This definitive Cider Sizing Guide is designed to help you find your exact fit, covering both standard sizes and the highly popular plus-size Cider Curve collection.

The Fit Verdict: Generally, Cider clothing runs true to size. Stretchy materials (knits, ribbed tops, oversized silhouettes) are highly forgiving, while structured fabrics (rigid denim, linen, corsets, and structured dresses) have very little stretch and tend to run slightly small. If you are between sizes or buying a fitted woven item, we always recommend sizing up.

Fit Tips by Category

Dresses & Outerwear

  • A-Line & Flowy Dresses: Highly forgiving in the hips. Focus on your bust and waist measurements for a perfect match.
  • Bodycon & Corset Dresses: Structured linings mean zero stretch. If you are busty or have broad shoulders, size up.

Jeans, Trousers & Skirts

  • High-Waist Pants: Cider’s high-waist rises can be tall. Check the rise measurement listed under "Size & Fit" on the item page.
  • Rigid Woven Denim: Pure cotton jeans will stretch slightly as you wear them, but they start snug. Size up if you prefer a relaxed waist.

How to Measure Yourself Like a Pro

To avoid return shipping, take your current measurements using a soft fabric measuring tape:

  • Bust: Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it flat and level.
  • Waist: Measure your natural waistline, which is typically the narrowest point above your hips and below your ribs (about an inch above your belly button).
  • Hips: Stand with your feet together and measure the widest part of your hips and rear.

Pro-Tip: Use the "Find Your Size" Widget

On every individual product page, Cider features an interactive sizing calculator. This tool cross-references your height, weight, and preferred fit with crowdsourced reviews from actual buyers who purchased that specific item. It is remarkably accurate!

Cider Sizing FAQs

What is the difference between XL and 0X at Cider?

While an XL generally corresponds to a US 12, a Curve 0X is tailored for a US 14. In addition to being slightly larger overall, the 0X pattern is cut with a more generous allowance in the bust, arms, and thighs compared to the standard XL.

Does Cider have free returns if the fit is wrong?

Cider has a very shopper-friendly policy. You can request a return within 14 days of delivery. The first return label for every order is completely free, making it easy to order two sizes and return the one that doesn't fit.
